# v-2: Portable narrator and NPC delivery profiles

**Severity**: HIGH — voice identity and delivery are persisted in an OpenAI-only, private-text-derived
shape, so provider switches reshuffle or reject voices and forks/imports silently lose the new state.
**Status**: Merged
**Branch**: `fix/v-2-portable-voice-profiles`
**Implementation commit**: `8456f89`
**Merge commit**: `ef304b7`

## Evidence

- `tts-providers.js:77-102` stores only provider/voice/free-text instructions; NPC assignment is
  permanently OpenAI and derives audible direction from private personality and quirks.
- `rpg-state.js:286-303` accepts arbitrary model-written tone text; `bundleVoice` at `:1038-1047`
  drops the planned numeric seed and finite mood.
- `rpg-engine.js:1261-1264` creates campaigns without `narrator_voice_json`; fork creation at
  `:2581-2584` also omits it and `:2634-2641` creates fresh NPC profiles instead of copying canon.

## Predicted observable failure

Switching an existing campaign from OpenAI to Grok cannot deterministically preserve speaker
identity, a fork can assign different voices to the same NPCs, and export/import loses the seed/mood
needed to reconstruct them. Arbitrary or private profile text can become audible delivery direction.

## What

Introduce the finite public delivery vocabulary and provider-portable profile state, then preserve
that state through every creation, legacy-read, fork, export, and import boundary without changing
the production audio request contract before v3.

## Approach

One ordered public delivery vocabulary now drives outline prompts, narration prompts, turn
validation, route compatibility, and stored mood validation. NPC profiles persist the campaign
creation index as `voiceSeed`; same-provider stored voices are honored, provider switches resolve
that seed through the active provider's NPC pool, and legacy JSON profiles accept an explicit
zero-based campaign ordinal. Newly assigned profiles contain no personality- or quirk-derived text.

Campaign creation persists the active provider's reserved narrator and provider-aware NPC profiles.
Forks copy narrator and NPC profiles verbatim. Bundle validation preserves only a raw numeric,
finite, non-negative seed (floored) and exact public mood; export/import production paths now retain
both. The current audio route remains on its existing request shape, with finite enum values rendered
as OpenAI-compatible `Mood:` / `Tone:` instructions until v3 replaces route resolution.
