Voice Narration (server side)

Speech goes through a provider seam; additional providers (e.g. voice cloning for unique NPC voices) register in tts-providers.js and appear here.
Voice narration is OpenAI-only for now (a provider seam is a recorded future topic). Accepted models: gpt-4o-mini-tts (instruction-steerable — accents/emotion via Voice Direction), tts-1, tts-1-hd (fixed character). Anything else silently falls back to gpt-4o-mini-tts. Players choose the voice and style in their own settings; the key and model live here.
